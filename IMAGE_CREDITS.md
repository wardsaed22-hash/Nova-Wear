Images were downloaded from the Unsplash image CDN, cropped (focal-point crops for product detail shots)
and encoded as **WebP** at 600 px and 1000 px widths for products, and at 700–1800 px for editorial imagery.

> NOVA WEAR is a fictional brand. The people and garments pictured are not affiliated with it, and product
> names, prices and descriptions in `src/data/products.js` are invented for demonstration purposes.

## Replacing images

To swap in your own photography, keep the same file names (or update `productImages()` in
`src/data/products.js`). Recommended product format: 4:5 portrait, WebP, 600 px and 1000 px wide.
